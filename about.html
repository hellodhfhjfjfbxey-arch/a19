About the school

Better studying, not longer studying.

Lessons is an independent website about learning how to learn.

We explain study methods from learning research in plain language, for students, parents and anyone learning something new. Our forgetting curve, flash cards and study timer make the ideas easy to try.

We do not sell courses, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
